# Geographic data notices

Memory Atlas ships a derived, simplified geographic context catalog. The
catalog is used only to choose named camera extents; it is not rendered as an
authoritative boundary layer. Exact source snapshots, hashes, selection rules,
and generation parameters are recorded in `catalog.provenance.json`.

## Natural Earth

Country and first-level administrative boundaries are derived from Natural
Earth vector data version 5.1.2. Natural Earth data is in the public domain.

Made with Natural Earth.

## United States national parks

United States national-park boundaries are derived from the National Park
Service Land Resources Division Boundary and Tract Data Service, retrieved
September 14, 2026. Source: National Park Service Land Resources Division.

Memory Atlas selected national-park features and simplified their geometry.
These derived extents are for photographic navigation, not legal, survey, or
land-management use.

## German national parks

German national-park boundaries are derived from the Bundesamt für Naturschutz
(BfN) Schutzgebiete WFS, layer `Nationalparke`, data status 2025, retrieved
September 14, 2026. © Bundesamt für Naturschutz (BfN) 2026. Source data is used
under the GeoNutzV.

Memory Atlas selected, transformed, and simplified the source geometry. The
derived extents are not suitable for planning purposes.
