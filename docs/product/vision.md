# Memory Atlas Product Vision

**Status:** Accepted long-term vision and naming rationale

**Last updated:** 2026-07-31

## Background

The original idea was to build an application for presenting travel photos—not as a traditional slideshow, but as an interactive experience that combines maps, routes, metadata, and contextual information.

As the concept evolved, it became clear that the vision was much broader.

## Core Insight

The application is **not about managing photos.**

It is about **preserving and exploring memories.**

A single memory or event may consist of many different artifacts:

- Photos
- Videos
- GPS tracks
- Locations
- Timestamps
- Notes
- Documents
- Audio recordings
- AI-generated summaries
- Links to related information

Photos and videos are simply different media types that capture the same moment.

## Why "Memory Atlas"?

The word **Memory** reflects the true purpose of the application far better than terms like *Photo* or *Journey*.

The application is not limited to travel. It should be equally useful for:

- Trips and vacations
- Hikes
- City walks
- Family gatherings
- Museum visits
- Conferences
- Personal projects
- Everyday life

The word **Atlas** represents exploration and orientation.

Instead of presenting memories only as folders or chronological albums, they can be explored through multiple perspectives:

- Map
- Timeline
- Places
- Media
- People
- Topics
- Relationships

An atlas is more than a collection of maps—it is a structured way to explore a world.

## Vision

**Memory Atlas is not a photo album.**

It is an interactive memory space where places, time, media, and context come together to tell richer stories than any single photo or video could tell alone.

The goal is not simply to browse files, but to rediscover experiences.
