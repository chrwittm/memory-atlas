## Installed-app verification

The exact final DMG was mounted read-only and installed into Applications with
`ditto --norsrc`; its installed app repeated deep/strict signature and identity
checks. Native packaged-app testing covered:

- natural folder order, caption and capture-time display, information toggle,
  previous/next navigation, ten-photo Page jumps, collection boundaries, photo
  zoom, pointer pan, and fitted-view restoration;
- the photo → divider → map focus loop, divider resizing, map-focused navigation,
  map Arrow pan and zoom ownership, and safe focus recovery after map close;
- all three `G` content modes, rendered tiles and attribution, individual and
  exact-coordinate grouped photo selection, the GPX track layer, persistent
  GPS/Zoom state, the primary `Z` cycle, and direct German geographic scopes;
- camera-scope preservation while navigating, fullscreen entry through `F`,
  first-Escape fullscreen exit with the map retained, and second-Escape map
  close; and
- no-GPS placeholder recovery, malformed-metadata isolation, quit/relaunch,
  and entry-screen focus recovery.

## Candidate defects found and resolved

Installed testing rejected two earlier unpublished 0.3.0 candidates:

- MA-BUG-004 deferred MapLibre indefinitely when an open no-GPS placeholder
  navigated to a located photo. Source and packaged regression checks now pass.
- MA-BUG-005 allowed a selected exact-coordinate group marker click to reach
  the underlying map layer and advance twice. The marker now stops the handled
  click, the regression test requires one selection, and the exact installed
  artifact visibly advances `2 / 4` → `4 / 4` → `2 / 4` on two clicks.

Neither rejected candidate was published or tagged. Their installed copies were
moved to the user's Trash and remain recoverable.

## Known limitation

The earlier owner report still covers unchanged offline photo behavior. Network
disconnection was not repeated for this build. MA-BUG-001, map tiles not always
recovering after an offline request, remains explicitly deferred and is not
claimed fixed.
